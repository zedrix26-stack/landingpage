Writing steps

About two minutes with a phone that has NFC.

8 steps
  1. Tap Copy URL above.
  2. Open an NFC writing app on your phone (NFC Tools works well).
  3. Select Write.
  4. Add a URL / URI record.
  5. Paste the TapX URL you copied.
  6. Hold the NFC card against the back of the phone.
  7. Tap Write and wait for success.
  8. Test it: tap the finished card on any phone — your profile must open directly.

Production guide

When you move the prototype to a real host.

hosting
  • Use HTTPS. Host the site on a real domain with a valid certificate — phones block file:// and plain http:// links on a tap.
  • Keep the URL stable. Once chips are written, do not move profile.html; a moved file breaks every card in the field.
  • Chips hold one URL each. Editing links or your photo later never requires rewriting the chip.
  • Print on PVC. Use a card printer or a service that prints CR80 cards; desktop printers rarely match PVC quality.
TapX never claims to write NFC chips itself and never stores your data on the chip — only this one public URL. Do not write sensitive personal information anywhere except your own profile page.
