How it works

From profile to physical card in three steps

The website builds your profile and its URL. Your NFC writing app stores that URL on the card. Your printer puts the design on PVC.

1

Create your profile

Add your name, photo, social links, contact details and portfolio. You instantly get a unique URL like profile.html?user=yourname.

2

Get your NFC card

Design the PVC card, print it, then write your TapX profile URL onto the NTAG chip with any NFC writing app.

3

Tap to share

Anyone taps the card — your profile opens instantly on their phone with all your links and contact details. No app to install, nothing to scan.
